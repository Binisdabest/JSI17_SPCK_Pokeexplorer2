About

For trainers, by trainers

PokéExplorer is a final project for Advanced Web Development, built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no backend, no database. Everything you see runs entirely in your browser.

Pokémon data comes live from PokéAPI, and trading card data from the Pokémon TCG API. Everything you do — PokéPoints, your collection, community posts, trainer profiles — is saved with localStorage, entirely on your device.

This is a fan project for educational purposes and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company.
