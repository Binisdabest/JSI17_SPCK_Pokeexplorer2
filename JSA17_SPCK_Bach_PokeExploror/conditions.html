Card Condition Guide

A card's grade is the single biggest factor in what it's worth. The quality bar below is a simplified stand-in for the corner/edge/surface wear graders actually look at.

GEM MINT · GM10

Gem Mint

Flawless under magnification — perfect centering, sharp corners, no surface wear at all. The rarest grade to achieve.

Typical value: +30–60% over Near Mint

MINT · M9

Mint

Nearly perfect — maybe one minor, hard-to-see flaw like a tiny print imperfection. Corners and edges still sharp.

Typical value: +10–20% over Near Mint

NEAR MINT · NM

Near Mint

The market baseline. Very light handling wear at most — barely noticeable without close inspection. Most "market price" quotes assume this grade.

Typical value: baseline (100%)

LIGHTLY PLAYED · LP

Lightly Played

Minor edge wear or light scratching visible on close look. Still displays well, still fully playable.

Typical value: −15–25% vs Near Mint

MODERATELY PLAYED · MP

Moderately Played

Noticeable whitening on edges/corners, visible scratches or scuffing on the surface. Clearly a "played" card.

Typical value: −35–45% vs Near Mint

HEAVILY PLAYED · HP

Heavily Played

Significant wear throughout — creasing, heavy edge whitening, surface scratching. Structurally intact but rough.

Typical value: −55–65% vs Near Mint

DAMAGED · DMG

Damaged

Tears, water damage, heavy creasing, writing, or missing pieces. Collector or playset value only.

Typical value: −75%+ vs Near Mint
